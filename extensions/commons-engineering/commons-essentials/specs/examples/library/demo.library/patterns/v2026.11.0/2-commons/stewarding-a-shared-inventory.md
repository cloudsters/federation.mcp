# Stewarding a Shared Inventory

> Stewards keep a shared inventory usable by checking items out, back in and repairing them.

### Section 1: Context

Households keep tools they use a few times a year, while neighbours who need them cannot afford their own.

### Section 2: Problem

Idle tools in every household stand against scarce access for those who need them.

### Section 3: Solution

A neighbourhood pools its tools in one place, lends them by a simple record, and asks a small return in care.

### Section 4: Implementation

Start with a shelf, a ledger and two keepers; add opening hours that fit working neighbours; repair before replacing.

### Section 5: Consequences

Fewer tools are bought, more get used, and neighbours meet over things they mend together.

### Section 6: Known Uses

**A neighbourhood in Ghent, Belgium:** a tool library in a former shop lends drills and ladders to a few hundred households and repairs them on Saturdays.

**A quarter of Nairobi, Kenya:** neighbours keep a shared box of garden tools at a community hall and lend them by a page in a notebook.

### Section 7: Cognitive Era

A shared ledger can show what is out and what is due, without tracking who borrows what beyond the loan itself.

### Section 8: Vitality

Tools come back clean, the shelf grows from donations, and the keepers change without the library closing.
